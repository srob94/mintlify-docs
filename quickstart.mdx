AgyDesk has you up and running quickly: sign up, choose a plan, and your cloud Chromium pod is ready immediately. From there you can watch your browser live, install the CLI, record a workflow by demonstration, and execute it as a 24/7 daemon — no servers to configure and no local browser to keep open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up and Choose a Plan">
    Go to [agydesk.com](https://agydesk.com) and create your account. During onboarding, select one of the three plans:

    | Plan | Price | Best For |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Starter** | $49 / mo | Solo users and explorers |
    | **Pro** | $129 / mo | Professionals running multiple workflows |
    | **Agency** | $349 / mo | Teams, client work, and high-volume automation |

    All plans include a dedicated cloud Chromium pod with a persistent browser profile and monthly credits for AI operations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Your Dashboard">
    After signup, you land on your AgyDesk dashboard. The dashboard is organized into seven tabs:

    - **Live Cloud Stream** — real-time noVNC view of your Chromium pod
    - **Prompt-to-Bot Engine** — AI bot generator
    - **Extension Manager** — install and manage Chrome extensions on your cloud pod
    - **Integrations Hub** — connect external services
    - **Team & RBAC** — invite members and manage permissions
    - **Cloud Settings** — configure your pod, choose BYOC infrastructure
    - **CDP Logs** — inspect Chrome DevTools Protocol events in real time
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Your Live Cloud Browser">
    Click the **Live Cloud Stream** tab. The noVNC stream loads instantly and shows your full Chromium browser running in the cloud. You can click, type, and navigate just as you would on a local machine — every action persists after you close the tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the CLI">
    Install the `agydesk` CLI globally with npm:

    ```bash
    npm install -g @agentdesk/cli
    ```

    Verify the installation and confirm your pod is reachable:

    ```bash
    agydesk status
    ```

    You should see your pod's CDP endpoint, noVNC URL, and storage paths confirmed as active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Your First Workflow">
    Start a recording session by giving your workflow a name:

    ```bash
    agydesk record my-workflow
    ```

    The CLI connects to your cloud pod's CDP endpoint and begins capturing every click and input. Switch to your dashboard's Live Cloud Stream tab and interact with the browser normally — log in to a site, fill a form, navigate pages. When you are done, return to your terminal and press **Enter** to stop recording.

    AgyDesk compiles your interactions into a TypeScript daemon worker file at `packages/daemon/src/workers/my-workflow.ts`, ready to run autonomously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Your Worker">
    List all available workers to confirm your recording was saved:

    ```bash
    agydesk workers
    ```

    Then execute your new worker:

    ```bash
    agydesk run my-workflow
    ```

    The daemon starts, connects to your cloud pod via CDP, and replays every recorded step. Leave it running — your cloud browser keeps executing the workflow 24/7 without any local process staying open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AI-powered operations consume credits from your plan's monthly allocation. Bot synthesis costs 50 credits, each browser step costs 1 credit, and vision/OCR operations cost 5 credits. Top up any time with a Boost ($20 / 2,000 cr), Growth ($50 / 6,000 cr), or Scale ($100 / 15,000 cr) credit pack.
</Note>
