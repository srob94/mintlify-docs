AgyDesk is a 24/7 cloud browser workstation that keeps a full Chromium browser running in the cloud so you never have to. Connect to your pod through a live noVNC stream or the Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP), build AI-generated automation bots with a single prompt, record workflows by demonstration, and share access securely with your team — all from one dashboard, with no local machine required.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up and Choose a Plan">
    Create your account at [agydesk.com](https://agydesk.com) and select the plan that fits your needs — Starter ($49/mo), Pro ($129/mo), or Agency ($349/mo). Each plan includes a dedicated cloud Chromium pod and a monthly credit allocation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Cloud Chromium Pod Spins Up Automatically">
    Once your plan is active, AgyDesk provisions a persistent Chromium browser in the cloud. Your browser profile is saved between sessions, so logins, extensions, and settings are always intact when you return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access via Live Stream or CDP">
    Open the **Live Cloud Stream** tab in your dashboard to see your browser in real time over noVNC (port 6080), or connect programmatically via the Chrome DevTools Protocol endpoint (port 9222) for full automation access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and Run Automations">
    Use **Prompt-to-Bot** to generate a worker from a plain-English description, record a workflow with `agydesk record`, or write a custom TypeScript daemon worker. Deploy it with `agydesk run` and let it work 24/7 — no local machine needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
