AgyDesk offers three subscription plans designed for individuals, growing teams, and enterprise automation operations. Each plan provisions a dedicated 24/7 cloud Chromium pod with a persistent browser profile volume, noVNC live stream, and CDP remote debugging access — the only difference is how much horsepower your pod gets and how many seats can collaborate on it.

## Signing Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit agydesk.com">
    Go to [agydesk.com](https://agydesk.com) and click **Get Started** in the navigation bar. You will be taken to the pricing page showing all three plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Click **Start with Starter**, **Start with Pro**, or **Start with Agency** depending on your needs. You can always change your plan later from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe checkout">
    Enter your email address and payment details in the Stripe-hosted checkout. All payments are processed securely — AgyDesk never stores your card data. Subscriptions are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pod auto-provisions">
    Within seconds of checkout completion, AgyDesk provisions your cloud Chromium pod and redirects you to your dashboard. Your pod will appear online in the **Live Stream** tab, ready for immediate use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Plans

To upgrade or downgrade your subscription, navigate to **Cloud Settings** → **Billing & Plan** in your dashboard and select a new plan. AgyDesk will immediately re-provision your pod with the new resource allocation (CPU, RAM, and storage). Your browser profile, installed extensions, and saved cookies are preserved across re-provisions because they are stored on your pod's persistent volume.

Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated for the remaining days in your current billing period.

<Note>
  Credits are not included in any subscription plan — they are purchased separately as one-time packs. Your plan keeps your pod running 24/7; credits pay for individual AI-powered actions such as Prompt-to-Bot synthesis, vision OCR, and HeyGen video rendering. See [Credits](/billing/credits) for pricing details.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Every new account receives a free credit allocation on signup so you can immediately try the Prompt-to-Bot engine and run your first autonomous browser workflow without purchasing a credit pack first.
</Tip>
