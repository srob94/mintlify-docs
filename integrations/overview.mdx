AgyDesk connects to the tools you already use — CRMs, data tables, communication platforms, AI media services, automation bridges, and cloud storage. All integrations are configured per-workspace from the **Integrations Hub** tab in your dashboard and are immediately available to every daemon worker and AI-generated bot running on your pod, with no additional configuration required in your worker code.

## How Integrations Work

Integrations are configured once at the workspace level and stored encrypted in AgyDesk's secrets vault. When a daemon worker or AI-generated bot needs to post a lead to HubSpot or upload a file to S3, it reads the integration credentials automatically from the pod environment — you do not need to hard-code API keys or webhook URLs in your worker scripts.

All integrations are scoped to your workspace. Team members with **Operator** role and above can use configured integrations in their workers. Only **Admins** and **Owners** can add, update, or remove integration credentials in the Integrations Hub.

## Connecting an Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations Hub">
    In your AgyDesk dashboard, click the **Integrations Hub** tab in the left sidebar. You will see all available integrations organized by category, each showing a **Connected** or **Disconnected** status badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your service">
    Locate the integration you want to connect. Click the **Connect** button on its card. Services using OAuth (HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets) will open an authorization popup. Services using API keys (Airtable, HeyGen, ElevenLabs, S3) will show a credential entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize or enter credentials">
    For OAuth services, complete the authorization flow in the popup and grant the requested permissions. For API key services, paste your key or token into the provided fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Click **Save**. AgyDesk will validate your credentials by making a test API call to the service. A green **Connected** badge confirms the integration is active and available to your workers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
